# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

> DRAFT template — finalize commercial terms with counsel. Targets below are proposed and must be confirmed against operational capacity.

This SLA supplements the Terms of Service between **VERTOFI PRIVATE LIMITED**
(CIN U62099TS2026PTC211039) and the Customer.

## 1. Availability target
Vertofi targets **[99.9%]** monthly availability for the core platform,
calculated excluding scheduled maintenance and Excluded Events (below).

> The platform is engineered toward higher availability (multi-AZ, autoscaling,
> graceful degradation). Commit only to a target your operations can sustain.

## 2. Definitions
- **Downtime:** the core service is unavailable for all of the Customer's users
  due to a fault within Vertofi's control.
- **Scheduled maintenance:** notified in advance ([24–72] hours) where feasible.
- **Excluded Events:** factors outside Vertofi's reasonable control, including
  third-party provider outages (banking/GST/payment/AI/cloud), force majeure,
  Customer-caused issues, and misuse.

## 4. Business Lifeguard (24×7)
Emergency support for GST/IT/TDS notices, fraud and disputes is available per the
applicable plan.

## 5. Service credits
For confirmed failure to meet the availability target, the Customer may request
service credits per the table in [Schedule B] (e.g., a percentage of the monthly
fee), as the sole and exclusive remedy.

## 6. Backups & recovery
Vertofi maintains backups with point-in-time recovery and documented RTO/RPO
targets; recovery procedures are tested periodically.

## 7. Maintenance
Routine maintenance is performed in low-traffic windows with prior notice where
practicable; emergency maintenance may occur without notice to protect security
or stability.

## 8. Reporting & claims
Service-credit claims must be submitted within [30] days of the incident with
reasonable supporting detail.
