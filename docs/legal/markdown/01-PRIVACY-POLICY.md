# Privacy Policy

**VERTOFI PRIVATE LIMITED** (CIN U62099TS2026PTC211039) ("Vertofi", "we", "us")
operates the Vertofi platform. This policy explains how we collect, use, share
and protect personal data.

**Effective date:** [Effective Date] · **Last updated:** [Date]

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to personal data we process about visitors, account holders,
authorised users, and the representatives of our business customers.

## 2. Data we collect
- **Account & identity:** name, designation, mobile, email, business details.
- **KYC / verification:** PAN, GSTIN, and documents you choose to upload.
- **Financial data you connect:** invoices, bank statements, GST filings, payroll
  and accounting records (processed to deliver the service).
- **Usage & device:** log data, IP address, device and session information.

## 3. How we use data (purposes)
To provide and operate the platform; automate accounting; compute financial
intelligence; prevent fraud and ensure security; comply with law; communicate
with you; and improve the service. We process data on the lawful bases of
**consent**, **performance of a contract**, and **legal obligation**.

## 4. Consent (DPDP)
Where we rely on consent, it is free, specific, informed and unambiguous, and may
be withdrawn at any time via the contact below. Withdrawing consent may limit
service functionality.

## 5. Sharing & sub-processors
We do not sell personal data. We share data with vetted sub-processors strictly
to deliver the service (e.g., cloud hosting, communications, OCR, payment, GST
and banking integrations). Each is bound by contractual confidentiality and
security obligations. A current sub-processor list is available on request.

## 6. Data localisation & transfers
Personal data is hosted in India. Any cross-border processing is limited to
permitted jurisdictions and safeguarded contractually.

## 7. Security
We apply encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, tenant isolation,
audit logging and other measures described in our Information Security Policy.

## 8. Retention
We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as
required by law (e.g., tax/accounting retention), after which it is deleted or
anonymised.

## 9. Your rights (DPDP)
Subject to law, you may request **access**, **correction**, **completion**,
**updating**, **erasure**, and **grievance redressal**, and may **nominate**
another person to exercise rights in case of incapacity/death. To exercise
rights, contact the Grievance Officer (Policy 07).

## 10. Children
The service is intended for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not
knowingly process children's data without verifiable parental consent.

## 11. Cookies
See our Cookie Policy (Document 09).

## 12. Changes
We may update this policy; material changes will be notified through the service
or by email.
