# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

> DRAFT template — finalize with counsel. Forms part of the Terms of Service.

This DPA is between **VERTOFI PRIVATE LIMITED** (CIN U62099TS2026PTC211039)
("Processor"/"Vertofi") and the Customer ("Controller") and governs the
processing of personal data by Vertofi on the Customer's behalf under the DPDP
Act, 2023.

## 1. Roles
The Customer is the Data Fiduciary/Controller of its data; Vertofi acts as a Data
Processor processing personal data only on documented instructions.

## 2. Subject matter & duration
Processing covers the personal and financial data the Customer submits or
connects, for the duration of the subscription plus any legally required
retention.

## 3. Nature & purpose of processing
Hosting, accounting automation, financial-intelligence computation, fraud and
security monitoring, and support — solely to provide the service.

## 4. Categories of data subjects & data
Data subjects: the Customer's personnel, vendors and customers. Data: identity,
contact, KYC, financial, transactional and usage data.

## 5. Processor obligations
Vertofi will: (a) process only on instructions; (b) ensure personnel
confidentiality; (c) implement appropriate technical and organisational security
measures (Document 06); (d) assist the Controller with data-subject requests and
breach notification; (e) not engage sub-processors except as permitted; and
(f) delete or return data on termination.

## 6. Sub-processors
The Customer authorises the sub-processors listed in our register (cloud,
communications, OCR, payments, GST/banking integrations). Vertofi remains
responsible for sub-processor compliance and will give notice of changes.

## 7. Security
Encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, tenant isolation, audit
logging, and the controls in the Information Security Policy.

## 8. Personal data breach
Vertofi will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a
breach affecting the Customer's data and assist with statutory notifications.

## 9. Data-subject requests
Vertofi will provide reasonable assistance to enable the Controller to respond to
access, correction, erasure, and grievance requests.

## 10. International transfers / localisation
Personal data is hosted in India; any transfer occurs only to permitted
jurisdictions with appropriate safeguards.

## 11. Audits
The Customer may, on reasonable notice and subject to confidentiality, verify
Vertofi's compliance through summaries, certifications, or a mutually agreed
audit.

## 12. Return & deletion
On termination, Vertofi will, at the Customer's choice, return or delete personal
data, subject to legal retention obligations.

## 13. Liability
Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the Terms of Service.
