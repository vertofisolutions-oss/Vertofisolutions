# 01 — Product Overview

## What Vertofi is

A **Predictive Accounting & Financial Intelligence Platform** for Indian MSMEs. Traditional software *records* history; Vertofi **records → analyzes → predicts → protects → advises**. The flagship promise is **Zero Data Entry**: clients never type a number — bills arrive via WhatsApp/email/photo, OCR + AI extract them, bank transactions auto-sync (Account Aggregator), and everything auto-categorizes and auto-reconciles.

- **Web dashboard = Mission Control.** The WhatsApp bot = the **24/7 CFO in your pocket**.
- Founder: Badiga Goutham.

## Vision & Mission

- **Vision:** India's most trusted Financial Intelligence Platform — automation, accuracy, peace of mind.
- **Mission:** Eliminate manual accounting, prevent financial losses, remove compliance stress, deliver real-time insight and predictive warnings.

## The 15 proprietary innovations (Vertofi Intelligence Suite™)

1. **Business Health Score (BHS™)** — 0–100 fitness score from expense discipline, tax compliance, cashflow, GST accuracy, payroll consistency, debt risk, profit leakage.
2. **Predictive Tax Warning System™** — forecasts GST/tax issues before penalties.
3. **Business Lifeguard™ (24×7)** — emergency desk for notices, fraud, disputes, crises.
4. **ProfitLeak Finder™** — detects duplicates, hidden charges, waste, unclaimed credits.
5. **Virtual Business Director™ (VBD)** — decision engine with financial-outcome simulations.
6. **WhatsApp Micro Accounting™** — capture via text/voice/image on WhatsApp.
7. **Financial Wellness Program™** — coaching for tax/expense/cashflow habits.
8. **Invisible Accounting™** — auto-sync bank, GST, TDS, payroll, POS, sales, WhatsApp.
9. **VendorTrust Score™** — vendor risk from GST history, disputes, defaults.
10. **Accounting Warranty+™** — Vertofi pays for penalties caused by Vertofi errors.
11. **MoneyMap Live™** — real-time inflow/outflow, profit zones, waste zones, seasonality.
12. **On-Demand Accountant™** — pay-per-use 10-minute help.
13. **Financial Black Box™** — audit-grade reconstruction of every change.
14. **Industry Benchmarks™** — anonymized peer comparisons.
15. **Zero-Data-Entry Accounting™** — eliminates manual input entirely.

Computation details for each: see [11-features-15-innovations.md](./11-features-15-innovations.md).

## Business model

Subscription tiers (2026 pricing): **Starter ₹1,499/mo**, **Growth ₹3,999/mo (most popular)**, **Pro ₹8,999/mo**, plus an Enterprise CFO Suite. Add-ons: extra users/businesses, OCR scans, WhatsApp Automation Pro, AI Forecasting Pro, API access, custom reports, training. 7-day free trial (Starter/Growth). Plan limits (businesses, users, invoices, OCR scans, storage, bank accounts) are **enforced at the API gateway** and the **plan-aware AI** layer.

## Target users

MSMEs, retail/trading, manufacturers, startups, freelancers, service providers — and the professional ecosystem around them (CA/CMA/CPA/CS/ACCA/CFA, accountants, lawyers, BHS intelligence firms). See [03-panels-and-hierarchy.md](./03-panels-and-hierarchy.md).
