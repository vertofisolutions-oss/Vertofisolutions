# Product & User Guide

**VERTOFI PRIVATE LIMITED** · CIN U62099TS2026PTC211039 · 3 June 2026

A plain-language guide to how each application is used.

## 1. Public website (landing)
Visitors learn what Vertofi does through product-driven content: the Business
Health Score methodology, the 15 innovations, how it works, pricing, and a
contact form. The **Services** menu links to the five public panels. Admin and
Teams are **not** listed (internal only). Buttons: **Get Started**, **Check BHS
Score**, **Login**.

## 2. Business application (clients)
1. **Register** with mobile + email (OTP verified).
2. **Onboarding (3 stages):** Registration → Financial Setup (with secure
   document upload) → Intelligence Setup (revenue, vendors, risk assessment,
   choose your professional). A confidence score is generated.
3. **Mission Control dashboard:** Business Health Score, cash position, cashflow
   risk, and MoneyMap Live. Panels show designed empty states until data is
   connected — never fabricated numbers.
4. Sensitive actions (e.g., GST filing) require a step-up OTP.

## 3. Professional panels
- **Associates (CA/CMA/CPA/CS/ACCA/CFA):** open an assigned client, view Health
  Score, exceptions and ledger; flag flaws.
- **Accountant Panel:** the associate's accounts team — view clients, ping the
  associate on issues.
- **BHS Intelligence:** view the Health Scores of granted clients plus their
  associated professional; ping the professional.
- **Legal Services:** manage assigned cases (auto-created from escalated GST/tax/
  fraud notices) and run AI notice analysis.

## 4. Internal admin portal (staff only)
The **Teams** panel: view-only access to assigned companies, flag flaws, request
elevated access. The **Admin console** has ten sections:
Overview · Billing Dues · AI & Cloud cost · Risks · CI/CD Pipelines · AWS status ·
**Database** (Excel-style browser with inline edit popups, column-secured) ·
**Observability** (embedded Grafana) · Governance (teams & grants) · Access Log.
Every admin view and edit is recorded to an immutable log.

## 5. WhatsApp CFO
Clients can capture invoices, voice notes and bills via WhatsApp and ask
financial questions; the assistant answers via the AI gateway and routes media
into the Zero-Data-Entry pipeline. Sensitive actions require OTP approval.

## 6. Authentication summary
- Clients: mobile OTP + email verification.
- Professionals & internal staff: OTP (password + TOTP for admins in full
  deployment).
- Step-up OTP for financial actions and sensitive downloads.

## 7. Support
In-app help, email (info@vertofi.com), and the Business Lifeguard 24×7 emergency
desk for notices, disputes and fraud.
